# Paper notes

## Working title

Uncertainty-Aware Mathematical Misconception Detection for Generalization to Unseen Middle-School Problems

## Claim discipline

Supported by this design: comparative performance on this benchmark, generalization across its held-out questions, calibration quality, and a simulated deferral curve.

Not supported without additional study: teacher workload reduction, learning-outcome improvements, K–12-wide performance, equitable performance across demographic groups, deployment readiness, or causal educational impact.

## Project success criterion

Publication is welcome but not required. The primary output is a technically substantial, public, reproducible repository that documents an original independent evaluation and its limitations. Similar academic work is a reason to refine attribution and differentiation, not to discard rigorous work. The differentiated contribution is the complete, inspectable implementation of problem-held-out evaluation, leakage audits, calibrated selective prediction, and negative-result reporting for this author-released benchmark.

## Planned paper outline

1. Motivation and scope.
2. Dataset/provenance, including authentic–synthetic composition and access terms.
3. Leakage-safe problem-held-out protocol.
4. Baselines and supervised model.
5. Calibration and simulated review results.
6. Error analysis, including confident failures.
7. Limitations, ethics, and reproducibility appendix.
